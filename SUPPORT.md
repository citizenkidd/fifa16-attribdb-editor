# Support

Bug reports can be submitted through the public repository's GitHub Issues page
or the maintained
[Evo-Web release thread](https://evoweb.uk/threads/fifa-16-attribdb-editor-v2.106510/).

Include:

- The selected database: AttribDB or AttribDB Gameplay
- What you were trying to do
- The complete error message
- Your browser name and version
- Whether the files were original FIFA files or an existing mod

Do not upload copyrighted game files or private mods unless you have permission
to share them.
